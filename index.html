Race Marshal Signals

FLAG SIGNALS ON THE BARUIPUR CIRCUIT

Green Flag

Track Clear / Race On

Full throttle racing permitted.

Yellow Flag

Caution Ahead

Slow down, no overtaking, hold position.

Red Flag

Session Stopped

Come to a safe, controlled immediate stop.

Blue Flag

Faster Kart Approaching

Yield racing line safely to the leader.

Checkered Flag

Finish / Heat Complete

Slow down and navigate to pit exit.
